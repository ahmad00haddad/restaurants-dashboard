**3. Understanding of the assignment**
Four people, four short films that show a real change in a working life: what they faced, what Skills4Work gave them, what they do now. The film should feel like their story, not a report, and still answer what the donor needs to see: skills, job or business, income, dignity.

**4. Methodology**
Pre-production (December)
- Call with the LI team per beneficiary to collect background and results.
- A short pre-visit or phone call with each beneficiary to build trust and choose the place and moments to film.
- One storyboard per story: opening image, interview questions, B-roll list, closing line, teaser idea. Sent for LI approval before filming.
- Informed consent from each beneficiary; nobody is filmed or shown against their wish; refugee safety respected (location details, faces, names on request).

Filming (one full day per beneficiary, December – January)
- Interview in their own workplace, two cameras, lav + boom sound, controlled light.
- B-roll of real work and daily life: hands, tools, customers, home, the road to work.
- Vertical (9:16) shots framed on set, not only cropped later.
- All travel and logistics across governorates covered by Faii House.

Post-production (January – February)
- Edit: 60-90 s 16:9 Full HD film per beneficiary + 15-30 s 9:16 teaser.
- Colour grading (DaVinci Resolve), sound clean-up and mix, licensed music.
- Arabic and/or English subtitles, LI / RDPP / Denmark logos, acknowledgements and disclaimers as per guidelines.
- Up to three revision rounds on consolidated feedback.
- Delivery: master + web-optimised MP4, plus all raw footage on drive / cloud link.
