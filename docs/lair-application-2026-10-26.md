Dear L-AIR team,

I am applying to the 2027 Artist-in-Residence program in Takagi. I read that L-AIR asks artists to learn from the tanners and craftspeople of Takagi and to make the process open, and that is the way I want to work.

1. Project title / name
"Takagi, by hand" (working title), a short documentary film by Ahmad Haddad.

2. Project overview / proposed theme
I would make a short film (about 15 minutes) about the tanners and leather craftspeople of Takagi, shot on anamorphic lenses so the grain of the leather, the hands and the workshops are seen at a scale people rarely see. Leather would also be a real part of the work: together with local craftspeople I would make a hand-made leather element (for example a screen, a camera-side object or a hanging piece that frames the film) and show it beside the film at the final presentation. I have no leather experience. I want to learn the work from the people who do it, and let that learning shape the film and the object. The theme is knowledge passed from hand to hand, and what leather tells about a town over a long time. The process would be shared openly, with short frames and notes posted during the residency.

3. Preferred season
Season 2 (1 April to 27 June 2027). Season 3 (1 July to 26 September 2027) would also work.

4. Biography
Nationality / current country of activity: Jordanian, working in Jordan (Irbid).
I am a director and cinematographer, and founder of FAII HOUSE, a small production house in Irbid. I started in photography in 2014 and have worked in cinema since 2018, independently since 2017, on films, documentaries and commercial work in Jordan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Tunisia, Morocco and Mauritania. Ezwitti won second prize in the Al Jazeera Documentary Competition 2022. I was cinematographer, editor and colorist on Bakeer, an official nominee in the Arab Short Films category and an official selection of the AIU Kuwait International Film Festival. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Yarmouk University.
