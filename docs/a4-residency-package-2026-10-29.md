## Project proposal (EN, ~430 words)

**Title:** *The Old Hands of the New South*

**Format:** short documentary, 12–15 minutes, plus a set of stills and a public screening at the end of the residency.

**Idea.** In Irbid, where I live, a city that has grown quickly in my lifetime, the people I like to film are the ones who keep something old alive inside something new: a baker who still opens before dawn in a street of new shops, a man who keeps pigeons on a rooftop between new buildings. Your call describes the same tension in Chengdu, between a long history and fast modernization on the southern side of the city. I want to find one person in or near the A4 district who does an old kind of work in this new place, and follow their day for several weeks.

**Method.** The first week I only walk, listen and drink tea with the "Super Agent" A4 pairs me with; the subject is chosen with that person, not imagined from outside. Then I film with one camera, natural light and a small sound kit, mostly at the same hours each day so the change of the place can be seen around a routine that stays the same. Nobody is filmed without clear consent, and the person whose story it is sees the cut first and decides what is shown. Interviews are translated with help from the A4 team and subtitled in Chinese and English.

**Participatory activities (two).**
1. *A morning walk with phones:* a group of local residents film their own street at dawn for one hour; I show basic framing and light, and we watch the material together the same day.
2. *First screening for the people in the film:* before the public showing, a small screening for the subject, their family and neighbours.

**Outcome.** The finished short film shown at A4 during the spring outcome display (May–June 2028), with selected stills and a talk about filming everyday work. A4 keeps the right to show the film in its programmes.

**Timeline (8 weeks).** Weeks 1–2: walking, meeting people, choosing the subject with the Super Agent, phone-walk event. Weeks 3–6: filming. Weeks 7–8: edit and colour on site, subject screening, public screening.

**Production grant use (RMB 10,000).** Local translator/fixer days, sound recordist for key days, transport inside Chengdu, hard drives, subtitling, screening set-up.

**About me.** Director and cinematographer from Irbid, Jordan; photography since 2014, cinema since 2018; founder of FAII HOUSE. I have filmed in Jordan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Tunisia, Morocco and Mauritania, for NGOs, cultural projects and brands. My documentary *Ezwitti* won second prize in the Al Jazeera Documentary Competition 2022.

## 项目方案 (CN)

**题目：**《新南城里的老手艺》

**形式：**12至15分钟的短纪录片，一组静态照片，以及驻留结束时的一场公开放映。

**构想。**我住在约旦的伊尔比德，这座城市在我成长的这些年里变化很快。我最喜欢拍的，是那些在新事物里守着旧东西的人：在一条新店林立的街上仍然天亮前开门的面包师，在新楼之间的屋顶上养鸽子的人。贵中心的征集说明写到，成都南边同样存在悠久历史与快速现代化之间的张力。我希望在A4所在的区域或附近，找到一位在这片新地方从事老行当的人，并跟拍他/她几周的日常。

**方法。**第一周我只走路、倾听，和A4为我配对的"超级经纪人"一起喝茶；拍摄对象由我们一起选定，而不是从外面想象出来。之后我用一台摄影机、自然光和简单的录音设备拍摄，尽量每天在相同的时间拍，让地方的变化围绕一个不变的日常显现出来。未经明确同意，不拍摄任何人；故事的主人第一个看到剪辑版本，并决定哪些内容可以出现。采访在A4团队的帮助下翻译，并配中英文字幕。

**参与式活动（两场）。**
1. *手机清晨散步：*一组本地居民用一小时拍摄自己街道的清晨；我讲基础的构图和光线，当天我们一起观看拍到的素材。
2. *为片中人举行的首映：*在公开放映之前，先为拍摄对象、家人和邻居举行一场小型放映。

**成果。**完成的短片在2028年春季成果展（5月至6月）于A4放映，并展出部分照片，举办一场关于拍摄日常劳动的分享会。A4可在其项目中放映该片。

**时间安排（8周）。**第1–2周：走访、认识人、与超级经纪人一起选定拍摄对象、举办手机散步活动。第3–6周：拍摄。第7–8周：现场剪辑和调色、为拍摄对象放映、公开放映。

**创作资助（人民币10,000元）用途。**本地翻译/向导、关键拍摄日的录音师、成都市内交通、硬盘、字幕制作、放映布置。

**关于我。**来自约旦伊尔比德的导演和摄影指导；2014年开始摄影，2018年进入电影行业；FAII HOUSE创始人。曾在约旦、阿联酋、沙特阿拉伯、突尼斯、摩洛哥和毛里塔尼亚为非政府组织、文化项目和品牌拍摄。纪录片《Ezwitti》获2022年半岛电视台纪录片比赛二等奖。
