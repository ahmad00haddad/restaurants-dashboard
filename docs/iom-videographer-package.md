Subject: Expression of interest, Consultant (Videographer), Climate Mobility Innovation Lab

Dear IOM CMIL and ROAP team,

I am Ahmad Haddad, a director and cinematographer based in Irbid, Jordan. For twelve years I have made human-interest films and documentaries, and I handle the whole chain myself: concept, script, filming, editing, colour, sound and subtitles.

What I understood from the call is that the four country offices will film, and the consultant has to turn their material into one clear story per country plus a regional film. That is the work I would focus on.

My approach:
1. Weeks 1 to 2, concepts and shared storyboards. One short concept note each for Fiji and Vanuatu (5 minutes) and the regional film (10 minutes), then concept support for the Philippines and Indonesia. Each concept starts from one person, one place and one funded action, so the Climate Catalytic Fund result is visible through a life and not through a list of activities.
2. A simple filming guide for each country office: shot list, interview questions, how to place the camera and record sound, consent and safeguarding steps, and the EU Humanitarian Aid and IOM visibility rules. This keeps the footage consistent and usable even when filmed by different teams.
3. Weeks 3 to 5, rough cuts from the footage received, with a review round with CMIL and ROAP at each stage, then final edits based on one consolidated set of comments.
4. Finishing. English subtitles, voice-over where needed, licensed music, 4K masters, 1920x1080 and 1080x1080 versions, clean versions without titles or music, separate audio tracks, four to five cut-downs of 30 to 60 seconds, shot compilations with date, place and a short description, and the subtitle script.
5. Dignity and safeguarding. Nobody appears without informed consent, the person decides what is shown, and sensitive scenes are cut or blurred on request.

I know the timeline to 30 November is tight, so I would agree the review dates in the first week and keep every rough cut small enough to approve quickly.

I have not worked in the Pacific or South-East Asia, and I will rely on the country offices for local context and language. My strength is shaping, directing from a distance, and finishing the film to the standard you describe.
