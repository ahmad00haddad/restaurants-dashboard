## The project to use (fits JFF short doc, AFAC Documentary, DFI development, Wijhat context)

Working title: "Before the Shops Open" (working title, change freely)

Logline (about 40 words): In Irbid, the hours between three and eight in the morning belong to a few people: the baker, the market porter, the man who hoses the street. A short documentary that follows one night turning into day in the city where Ahmad lives.

Format: documentary short, about 20 minutes, Arabic, observational, no interviews to camera. Shooting in Irbid over about 6 nights/mornings.

Synopsis (about 150 words): The city that most people know awake is rarely seen while it prepares to wake up. The film follows three people at work before the shops open: a bread baker at the oven, a porter at the vegetable market, and a street cleaner. Each is filmed in long, patient takes in the low light of the hour, close to hands, steam, crates and water on stone, with the sound of the city slowly arriving. There is no narration. A few lines they say while working carry the story. The film ends as the first shutters go up and the street fills, and the three disappear into an ordinary day that depends on them. It is a small, direct film about unseen work and the place where the director lives and works.

Approach: shot by the director as cinematographer, small crew, available light and cinema-lens look, edited and graded in-house at Faii House in Irbid.

Why the director: Ahmad Haddad, director and cinematographer, founder of FAII HOUSE (Irbid), working in photography since 2014 and in cinema since 2018, independently since 2017; Ezwitti won second prize in the Al Jazeera Documentary Competition 2022; cinematographer, editor and colorist on Bakeer (Arab Short Films category nomination).

Not stated, to complete by Ahmad before use: characters' consent (names, who agrees to be filmed), exact budget (use the quote builder at faiihouse.lovable.app/quote-builder/index.html; solo documentary around 300 JOD per his own range, grants usually ask a fuller budget), shoot dates.

Links: behance.net/ahmad00haddad , ahmadhaddad.lovable.app , faiihouse.lovable.app
