# Soft visibility plan: press, cinema magazines, platforms

Goal: the world sees Ahmad and knows he exists. Quiet, smart, never salesy. We offer a story or a work; we never ask for a job.
Status: targets below are leads to verify on each site's own submission page (search could not confirm every process). Nothing is sent without Ahmad's approval.

## Pitch rules
- Give them a story, not a service: one film, one frame set, one honest angle. Never "available for hire" in a press pitch.
- Frame grabs from the final film (10+ stills), title, one-paragraph description, release/festival date, credit line. Ready once, reused.
- One pitch per outlet, personal, short. No mass mailing. Wait; a reply can take months.
- Press tone: the Shot magazine photo in 2016 → ten years later, directing and shooting across 6 countries. True, human, not a boast.

## Story angles (pick one per outlet)
1. **Ten years after the first published photo**: from a Shot magazine #34 print (2016) to a DOP for UN Women, UNICEF and L'Occitane. Personal-journey piece.
2. **Films that travelled**: short films shot in Jordan that reached festivals (Marrakech, Kuwait, Arab short films nomination). Craft piece: lighting choices, one scene. Pick the film to match the outlet; no single film is the lead.
3. **Qalby Etmaan, 5 countries**: shooting a TV show across Tunisia, Morocco, Mauritania, Jordan, UAE. Behind-the-scenes piece.
4. **Philosophy** (from /about): cinema that breathes the clay of our society, not fast-fame visuals. Interview / opinion piece.

## Press and magazines (verify each submission page)
- **Canadian Cinematographer (CSC)**: open to pitches. Needs 10+ stills/frame grabs from the finished project, title and description, release or festival date, approval to publish. Replies take ~2 months.
- **British Cinematographer**: Europe's cinematography magazine, runs DP interviews. Find the editor contact; pitch angle 1 or 2.
- **ICG Magazine**: mainly members of IATSE Local 600; low fit, skip.
- **A Shot magazine (Egypt)**: Arab cinema publication; check whether it takes submissions or interviews.
- **Arab film press**: Screen Daily (Arab Stars of Tomorrow type lists), Jordanian outlets (7iber culture section), Royal Film Commission news. Look for a culture editor, not a generic inbox.
- **Shot magazine itself** (published him in 2016): a short thank-you plus an update is the warmest door he has.

## Platforms (set up once, then leave alone)
- **Vimeo**: one clean reel + a few films, aim for a Staff Pick later.
- **Short of the Week / Directors Notes**: submit a publicly releasable film chosen per platform.
- **ShotDeck**: frames as a visual reference library; DPs are found through it.
- **Mandy, ProductionHub, Staff Me Up**: crew listing profiles; free to create. For commercial work abroad.
- **LinkedIn**: one post a month about one project with its story. No "proud to announce".
- **IMDb**: make sure Bakeer, Error 017, Ezwitti, Road Safety, Qalby Etmaan are credited.
- **Festivals**: submit to Jordan Short Film Festival (Amman), Amman International Film Festival, Fujifilm Short Film Festival Dubai (2026 edition 12–13 Dec, shorts up to 30 min; check FilmFreeway).
